## Installation Steps for Oracle VirtualBox.
### Step 1 - Go to your browser and type "oracle virtual box download", and click on the oracle website. <img width="1337" height="546" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-18-112218" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7b7af1ab-8414-411b-89b6-0e08606d9956" />
### Step 2 - When you enter Oracles's website, you have two sections. First, to your right, accept and download the extension file for the app. Then to your left, download the application file suitable for your pc.<img width="1300" height="314" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-19 032256" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c45c8090-5361-4197-818f-53ced332ae1a" />
### Step 3 When your done downloading both files, click on the application file, and follow the steps to install the file. Once you do click finish, and enter the application. <img width="1366" height="768" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-19 030744" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/96ee07a8-a29f-4048-b4c7-9d20fc9ed9f5" />
### Step 4 - When inside the appplication, on the taskbar to your left, select extension, and click the install button above. You will be redirected to your download folder where you will add the extension pack initially downloaded. <img width="1363" height="368" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-13 115653" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0d524c9c-34c7-4110-8914-c39953f12155" />

 ## Installation steps for Kali Linux.
 ### Step 1 - Enter your browser and type 'Kali Linux download'. <img width="1274" height="505" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-13 115845" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f64150b2-2c3e-4e62-9082-3a4597848abf" />
 ### Step 2 - When you enter the website, you have two options; Installer images and virtual machines. Click on the virtual machine option. <img width="1329" height="355" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-19 031219" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c7494f32-d850-44e1-9537-7402f6f2b899" />

When you finish extraxtion, enter the file with 3kb of space. <img width="1007" height="349" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-13 120229" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ce5d2236-c57c-468b-b913-b8380ad5a482" />

If you want to configure anything on for the operating system, select the settings section above. <img width="1366" height="768" alt="Screenshot 2026-03-19 040145" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bd6ee117-6ad1-4b90-999f-ba3d0e0ee30e" />
